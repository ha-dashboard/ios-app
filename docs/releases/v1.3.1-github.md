# HA Dashboard 1.3.1

These are the public notes for the v1.3.1 tag workflow.

## French translation

HA Dash now speaks French, translated by @psolyca in
[#27](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/pull/27). The app follows your
iPad's language, and Settings has a language picker if you want a different
one. Entity states and modes keep following your Home Assistant user's
language, as in 1.3.0. Want HA Dash in your language? See
[docs/translations.md](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/blob/main/docs/translations.md).

## New and improved cards

- **Clock card:** Home Assistant's built-in `clock` card renders natively, in
  digital and analog styles, with seconds, date formats, time zones and the
  analog face options ([#21](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/21)).
- **Button cards** now look like Home Assistant's: a large centred icon with
  the name below. Buttons with no entity (just an icon, a name and an action)
  now appear and run their action
  ([#26](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/26)).
- **Media control card:** a power button, controls that follow what your
  player supports and its current state (off shows only power), and a ⋮
  button for details. The details view gets the same treatment, and its
  buttons render on older iOS versions
  ([#24](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/24)).
- **Weather forecast card:** `show_current`, `show_forecast`, `forecast_type`,
  `forecast_slots`, `round_temperature` and `secondary_info_attribute` are
  honoured ([#20](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/20)), and a
  forecast-only card no longer cuts off its last day.
- **Headings and badges:** every heading in a section is shown, not just the
  first; view badges appear above the sections; heading cards show their
  badges; and badges support hold and double-tap actions
  ([#23](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/23)).
- **Timestamp sensors** show as times ("In 10 hours", or with `time_format`,
  e.g. `08:28:21`) instead of "2,026", and a blank `name: " "` hides the name
  as in Home Assistant ([#22](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/22)).

## Sections layout

Sections views now match Home Assistant more closely
([#25](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/issues/25)):

- `column_span` is supported, so a section can be 2 or more columns wide.
- Cards are clamped to Home Assistant's minimum and maximum widths for their
  type (a tile is never narrower than half a section), which removes gaps.
- Cards with no `grid_options` use Home Assistant's default widths.

## Fixes

- Light tiles without features are no longer twice as tall as other tiles.
- Cards whose height depends on their state (lights, media players, alarm
  keypads, badge rows) resize when the state changes.
- The connection to Home Assistant now backs off when the server is down
  (2, 4, 8, 16 seconds, then 30–60 seconds) instead of retrying every
  2 seconds, and reconnects as soon as it is back.
- The on-device entity cache writes about 60× less often (at most every
  5 minutes instead of every 5 seconds, and only when something changed),
  cutting flash wear.
- The kiosk-mode banner is now translated.

## Which build to install

- **iOS 15 and later:** update from the
  [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/ha-dash/id6759347912).
- **iOS 9.3.3 – 14.x:** the App Store only offers 1.2.5 on these versions, so
  **1.3.1 is only available from the universal IPA attached to this release**,
  or from the permanent link
  [HADashboard-universal.ipa](https://github.com/ha-dashboard/ios-app/releases/latest/download/HADashboard-universal.ipa).
  Install it on a jailbroken device with AppSync Unified or a similar tool, or
  sideload it with a tool such as Sideloadly. With a free Apple ID, sideloaded
  apps must be re-signed every 7 days.

The universal IPA supports armv7 and arm64, with a minimum of iOS 9.0 in both
slices. Local Camera Stream still requires iOS 10.3.3 or later.

When reporting a bug, include your device, iOS version, and app version
(Settings → version).
